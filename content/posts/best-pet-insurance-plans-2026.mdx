## How to Choose the Right Plan

### Step 1: Know Your Breed's Risk Profile
Research your breed's common health issues. Golden Retrievers have high cancer rates (60%+); Bulldogs face respiratory issues and joint problems; Siamese cats are prone to respiratory and eye conditions. The higher your breed's baseline health risk, the more value you get from comprehensive coverage.

### Step 2: Pick Your Reimbursement Model
All three insurers offer **actual cost reimbursement** (they reimburse a percentage of whatever your vet charges, not a "benefit schedule" of preset amounts). This is the model you want—benefit schedule plans (common in cheaper policies) can leave you with huge out-of-pocket expenses.

### Step 3: Run the Math on Deductibles
Annual deductibles reset each year. A $100 deductible sounds better than $500, but if you only file one claim per year for a routine illness, you may never recoup the higher premium. For catastrophic illness or accident plans, lower deductibles usually win.

### Step 4: Get Multiple Quotes
Premiums vary by zip code, breed, age, and deductible. A plan that's cheapest in one city may not be cheapest in another.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is pet insurance worth it?**
A: For most owners, yes—especially for young pets enrolled before any conditions develop. The break-even point is typically one moderate illness or accident per policy year. Cancer, orthopedic surgery, or any specialist referral almost always exceeds a full year's premiums in a single visit.

**Q: Does pet insurance cover pre-existing conditions?**
A: No insurer covers pre-existing conditions (conditions diagnosed or showing symptoms before the policy start date). This is why enrolling young and healthy is critical.

**Q: Can I use any vet?**
A: Yes—all three plans (Lemonade, Fetch, Healthy Paws) let you use any licensed vet in the U.S., including specialists and emergency clinics. You pay the vet, then submit for reimbursement.

**Q: What's the best pet insurance for a puppy?**
A: Lemonade offers the lowest premiums for young dogs and the easiest enrollment. Healthy Paws is a close second if you prioritize unlimited lifetime coverage from day one.

**Q: Does pet insurance cover dental?**
A: Fetch includes dental illness in its base policy. Lemonade offers it as an add-on. Healthy Paws does not cover dental illness (only dental accidents).

**Q: How long is the waiting period?**
A: Typically 2 days for accidents and 14–15 days for illnesses. Orthopedic conditions may have a 6-month waiting period unless waived by a wellness exam.

**Q: Can I insure a senior pet?**
A: Yes, but premiums are significantly higher and some plans stop accepting new enrollments after age 10–14 (varies by insurer). Enroll before age 7 to lock in reasonable rates.
