## Pet Water Fountain Buying Guide

### Why Moving Water Matters for Cats

Cats have a low thirst drive — they evolved in arid environments and got most of their hydration from prey. Domesticated cats often don't drink enough still water, especially when fed dry kibble.

Fountains help because:
1. Moving water stays oxygenated and tastes fresher
2. Cats instinctively trust running water more than still water
3. The sight and sound of moving water attracts cats to drink more frequently

Studies suggest cats that drink from fountains have 15-30% higher daily water intake than those drinking from bowls. For cats prone to UTIs or kidney issues, this can be genuinely life-changing.

### Plastic vs Ceramic vs Stainless Steel

**Plastic:** Cheapest, lightest, most options. Downside: scratches harbor bacteria. Replace every 1-2 years.

**Ceramic:** Heavier, more stable, won't scratch as easily. Dishwasher safe. Better for bacteria control. Mid-range price.

**Stainless steel:** Most hygienic, fully dishwasher safe, lasts longest. Best for cats with chin acne (often caused by plastic bacterial growth). Most expensive.

### How Often to Clean

- **Weekly:** Rinse the fountain bowl, wipe the pump
- **Monthly:** Full disassembly, soak parts in diluted white vinegar, scrub pump impeller
- **Every 2-4 weeks:** Replace the carbon filter

Skipping cleaning leads to biofilm (slimy pink/orange residue), algae growth, and reduced pump life.

### Pump Maintenance

The pump is the heart of any fountain. To extend its life:
- Never let the reservoir run dry (the pump burns out)
- Clean the impeller monthly (it's where debris accumulates)
- Use filtered or distilled water if your tap water is hard — minerals clog pumps faster

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do dogs like water fountains?**
Many dogs love them! Dogs drinking more water helps prevent UTIs, kidney stones, and overheating. Dogs who prefer running water from outdoor faucets or garden hoses often love fountains indoors.

**Q: How loud are pet water fountains?**
Most quality fountains run at 30-40 dB — quieter than a refrigerator hum. If yours is noisy, usually the pump is clogged or the water level is too low.

**Q: Can cats get their whiskers wet from a fountain?**
Cats with "whisker fatigue" may prefer wider or shallower bowls. Most fountains have a wide basin for whisker-comfortable drinking.

**Q: How often do I replace the filter?**
Every 2-4 weeks for activated carbon filters in most fountains. Some stainless models use a simple foam filter that lasts longer. Always follow your specific fountain's guidelines.
