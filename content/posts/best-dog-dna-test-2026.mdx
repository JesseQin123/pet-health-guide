## Embark vs. Wisdom Panel: Side-by-Side Deep Dive

### Breed Accuracy

Both tests perform excellently on dogs with straightforward mixed ancestry (Lab + Golden, Beagle + Boxer, etc.). Where they diverge:

- **Unusual or rare breeds:** Embark's higher SNP count wins here. Owners of Xoloitzcuintlis, Catahoulas, and other less-common breeds consistently report Embark is more accurate.
- **Very mixed "mutt" dogs:** Embark handles multi-generational mixed breeds better. Wisdom Panel sometimes rounds uncertain ancestry to broader categories.
- **Common purebreds:** Both are essentially equal — you'll get the same answer from either test.

### Health Screening

Embark screens for 50 more conditions than Wisdom Panel and provides cleaner, more actionable reports. Their results include a "what to discuss with your vet" section that non-expert dog owners find valuable.

Wisdom Panel's health reports are competent but slightly less detailed. For most casual pet owners, 200 conditions is more than enough.

### Traits

Wisdom Panel wins on sheer number of traits tested (50+ vs. Embark's 35+). Their behavioral tendency data is particularly interesting, though it should be taken as genetic probability — not destiny.

### Turnaround

Both take 2–4 weeks. Wisdom Panel edges slightly faster in some reviews, but it varies by volume.

## What Dog DNA Tests Can't Tell You

DNA tests reveal genetic predispositions — not certainties. A dog that tests positive as a carrier for hip dysplasia won't necessarily develop hip problems. Environment, diet, weight management, and exercise all play major roles.

Think of DNA results as a conversation starter with your vet, not a diagnosis. Share your results with your veterinarian at your next appointment — both Embark and Wisdom Panel generate shareable PDF reports designed for exactly this purpose.
