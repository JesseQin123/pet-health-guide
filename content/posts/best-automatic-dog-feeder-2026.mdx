## Why Consistent Feeding Matters

Dogs thrive on routine. Irregular feeding schedules can:

- **Trigger anxiety and begging behavior**, especially in food-motivated breeds
- **Cause overeating** when meals are delayed, increasing bloat risk in deep-chested breeds
- **Disrupt housetraining** in puppies whose elimination timing is tied to meals
- **Worsen weight management** in dogs on calorie-restricted diets for obesity

A quality automatic feeder removes the human variable and delivers meals on the same schedule, 365 days a year.

## What to Look For Before Buying

**Portion accuracy:** Test your feeder's dispense accuracy with your specific kibble size. Irregular shapes (triangles, bones) jam more than round or cylindrical kibble.

**Hopper capacity:** A 6-liter feeder holds roughly 24 cups of dry kibble — about 2 weeks for a 30-pound dog eating 1.5 cups/day.

**Power backup:** Essential if you live somewhere with frequent power outages or if your dog is on a medical feeding schedule.

**Kibble compatibility:** Most feeders work with dry kibble 0.25" to 1.5" in diameter. Semi-moist food clogs most automatic feeders and should be avoided.

## FAQ

**Q: Can automatic dog feeders handle wet food?**
A: No — virtually all automatic feeders are designed for dry kibble only. Wet food clogs the auger mechanism and creates bacterial growth in the hopper. Use a separate timed bowl for wet food.

**Q: Are automatic feeders good for puppies?**
A: Yes, with careful setup. Puppies often need 3–4 small meals per day, which automatic feeders handle well. Use PetSafe for its 12-meal flexibility and smallest portion size (1/8 cup).

**Q: What happens if the feeder jams or malfunctions while I'm away?**
A: PetSafe sends jam notifications via app. For non-WiFi feeders, set up a pet camera so you can visually confirm feeding. Always ensure your dog has access to fresh water regardless of feeder status.

**Q: How do I transition my dog to an automatic feeder?**
A: Run the feeder alongside your normal schedule for 1–2 weeks while you're home, so your dog learns to associate the dispensing sound with mealtime. Gradually shift to the feeder's schedule before leaving your dog home alone with it.
