## What Is Innovet CBD Oil?

Innovet's flagship product is their **PurCBD+ oil**, a CBD isolate tincture formulated specifically for pets. Unlike full-spectrum products (which contain multiple cannabinoids and trace THC), Innovet uses an isolation process to extract only CBD — removing all other compounds including THC.

The base is MCT oil derived from coconuts, which improves CBD absorption and adds a neutral carrier that most dogs tolerate well.

Innovet also makes a **EASE hemp extract** formula, which blends CBD with omega fatty acids for joint-focused support — marketed particularly toward older dogs with arthritis or mobility challenges.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros

**1. Certified THC-free.** For owners who need absolute certainty about zero THC exposure, Innovet's isolate formula is the most reliable option at this price point.

**2. Affordable entry price.** The 125mg bottle at ~$19.99 is one of the most accessible starting points in the CBD pet market.

**3. Rigorous third-party testing.** Independent lab verification of potency, THC, pesticides, and heavy metals is thorough and publicly available.

**4. Clean ingredient list.** MCT oil base, CBD isolate, and minimal additives. No unnecessary fillers.

**5. 30-day money-back guarantee.** Lowers the risk for first-time buyers.

**6. Vet-developed formulas.** Innovet works with veterinary consultants in formula development, adding credibility to their dosing guidelines.

### Cons

**1. Isolate only — no full-spectrum option.** The absence of minor cannabinoids and terpenes means no entourage effect. Some research suggests full-spectrum CBD may be more effective for anxiety and pain than isolate alone.

**2. Lower potency ceiling.** 500mg is the max standard bottle size. Large breeds (75+ lbs) needing higher doses will either need to dose more frequently or switch to a higher-potency brand. Compare to HolistaPet's 3,000mg option.

**3. Limited product range.** Mainly tinctures and soft chews. No topical, no capsules.

**4. COA navigation.** Lab results are accessible but not directly linked on individual product pages — minor friction for verification-minded shoppers.
