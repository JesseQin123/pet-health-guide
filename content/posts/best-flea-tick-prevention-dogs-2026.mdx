## Important Safety Note: The Isoxazoline Warning

NexGard, Bravecto, and other "isoxazoline" class drugs carry an FDA black box warning that they may cause neurological side effects (muscle tremors, ataxia, seizures) in rare cases — especially in dogs with a history of seizures.

This doesn't mean they're dangerous for most dogs. The FDA reviewed them and they remain approved. But if your dog has a seizure history, discuss with your vet before starting any isoxazoline product.
