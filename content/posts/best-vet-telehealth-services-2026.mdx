## What Is Vet Telehealth (And When Does It Work)?

Vet telehealth connects you via video, chat, or phone with a licensed veterinarian — no clinic visit required. It's ideal for:

- **Triage**: "Is this an emergency or can it wait?"
- **Minor symptoms**: Mild vomiting, soft stools, small wounds, itchy skin
- **Medication refills** (where legally allowed)
- **Behavioral concerns**: Anxiety, aggression, house-training questions
- **Post-op follow-ups** with your primary vet

It does **not** replace in-person care for physical exams, lab work, X-rays, surgeries, or serious illness. The best services are upfront about this limitation.

## Are Vet Telehealth Services Worth It?

The short answer: yes, for most pet owners.

A single after-hours ER visit for a non-emergency issue can cost $200–$500 in exam fees alone. One avoided ER trip pays for a year of Fuzzy membership or a dozen Vetster consults. More importantly, telehealth triage gives you peace of mind: you're not gambling on whether to go in.

That said, telehealth is a complement to in-person vet care, not a replacement. Maintain a relationship with a primary vet for annual exams, vaccines, and anything requiring physical examination.
