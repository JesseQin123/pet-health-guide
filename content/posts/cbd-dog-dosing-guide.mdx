# CBD Dosing Guide for Dogs: How Much CBD to Give Your Dog

CBD is one of the most common questions new pet owners ask: *How much CBD do I give my dog?* Get the dose too low and you won't see results. Get it too high and your dog may be overly drowsy. This guide gives you a science-backed starting framework and shows you how to dial in the right dose for your individual dog.

---

## The Short Answer: CBD Dosing Basics

**Starting dose**: 0.25 mg of CBD per pound of body weight, twice daily.

That's the baseline most veterinary practitioners recommend for dogs new to CBD. From there, you adjust up or down based on your dog's response over 2–4 weeks.

For most conditions, the **therapeutic range** is **0.25–0.5 mg/lb twice daily**. Research on osteoarthritis in dogs used 2 mg/kg (approximately 0.9 mg/lb) twice daily with excellent results. Adjust based on response.

## How to Dose CBD Oil for Dogs

CBD oil is the easiest format to dose precisely because you can measure to the exact milliliter.

### Step 1: Check your bottle's concentration

CBD oil concentration is listed as total mg per bottle or per ml. Common formats:
- **300 mg per 30 ml bottle** = 10 mg per ml
- **600 mg per 30 ml bottle** = 20 mg per ml
- **1200 mg per 30 ml bottle** = 40 mg per ml

### Step 2: Calculate your ml dose

`ml per dose = your dog's dose (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/ml)`

**Example**: 50 lb dog, starting dose of 12.5 mg, using 300 mg/30 ml oil (10 mg/ml):
- 12.5 mg ÷ 10 mg/ml = **1.25 ml per dose**

Most droppers have measurement markings at 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1 ml.

### Step 3: Administer properly

For best absorption:
- **Under the tongue (sublingual)**: Fastest absorption — 15–45 minutes to effect
- **Mixed into food**: Absorbed through digestion — 45–90 minutes, slightly lower bioavailability
- **Directly in mouth**: Most dogs will accept this if the carrier oil is palatable (MCT coconut oil in quality products is mild-tasting)

## Dosing by Condition

### Anxiety (Separation, Noise Phobia, Social)

**Daily maintenance**: 0.25 mg/lb twice daily. Consistent daily dosing builds baseline calm better than situational dosing alone.

**Situational boost**: For predictable triggers (thunderstorms, car trips, vet visits), give an additional dose 30–60 minutes before the event.

For more on managing dog anxiety naturally: [Natural Anxiety Remedies for Dogs](/blog/natural-anxiety-remedies-for-dogs)

### Arthritis and Joint Pain

**Research-backed dose**: 2 mg/kg twice daily (approximately 0.9 mg/lb). This is the dose used in the 2018 Cornell University study that showed significant reduction in pain scores and increased mobility.

Start at 0.25 mg/lb and titrate up over 4–6 weeks, watching for mobility improvement.

Related: [How to Help a Dog with Arthritis Naturally](/blog/how-to-help-a-dog-with-arthritis-naturally)

### General Wellness/Preventive

0.1–0.25 mg/lb once or twice daily. Many pet owners use CBD at the low end for overall immune support and homeostasis, similar to how humans use a daily supplement routine.

### CBD for Cats

Cats are not small dogs — they metabolize cannabinoids differently. Always use products formulated specifically for cats. Starting dose for cats is typically 0.1–0.2 mg/lb twice daily, lower than the dog equivalent.

## How to Adjust Your Dog's CBD Dose

### The Titration Method

1. **Start at 0.25 mg/lb twice daily**
2. **Maintain for 2 weeks** — this is enough time to assess basic response
3. **Evaluate**: Are you seeing improvement? Is your dog drowsy?
   - If **no improvement**: increase dose by 25% and wait another 2 weeks
   - If **drowsy**: decrease dose by 25% — drowsiness means you've hit or exceeded the right threshold for that dog
   - If **improvement without drowsiness**: maintain or continue titrating to the higher end of the therapeutic range
4. **Maximum titration**: Most dogs reach optimal response before 0.5 mg/lb twice daily. Higher doses are safe but rarely provide additional benefit.

### Signs the Dose Is Working

- Reduced anxiety behaviors (less pacing, whining, destructive behavior)
- Improved mobility — getting up more easily, more willing to exercise
- Better sleep — settling down more calmly at night
- More social engagement and playfulness in anxious dogs

### Signs the Dose Is Too High

- Drowsiness that interferes with normal activity
- Decreased appetite (usually temporary with adjustment)
- Loose stools (typically related to the carrier oil, not CBD itself — try giving with food)

## Is CBD Safe at These Doses?

Yes. CBD has a wide safety margin in dogs. The most comprehensive safety study (CSU, 2020) tested doses up to **4.5 mg/kg (approximately 2 mg/lb)** — about four times the upper therapeutic dose — with no adverse effects on serum chemistry, liver enzymes, or physical exam findings.

**Important caveats:**
- Always use hemp-derived CBD (not cannabis with THC). THC is toxic to dogs.
- Verify COA shows <0.3% THC or 0% THC
- If your dog is on medications (NSAIDs, seizure medications, immunosuppressants), CBD can affect drug metabolism via the cytochrome P450 system — consult your vet before starting

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long before I see results from CBD?**
For anxiety: you may notice effects within 30–60 minutes of the first dose. For chronic conditions like arthritis, consistent use for 2–4 weeks is needed to see full benefit. Read our complete timing guide: [How Long Does CBD Take to Work in Dogs?](/blog/how-long-does-cbd-take-to-work-in-dogs)

**Should I give CBD with food?**
Either way works. CBD with food slows absorption but may improve GI tolerance. Sublingual (under the tongue) administration gives faster onset if needed.

**Can I give my dog too much CBD?**
At normal doses, no. CBD has not caused toxicity in dogs even at doses far above therapeutic range. The main side effects of excess dosing are drowsiness and possibly loose stools. These resolve when dose is reduced.

**How often should I give CBD?**
Twice daily (every 12 hours) is the standard protocol. This maintains more consistent blood levels than once-daily dosing.

**Can I give my dog CBD every day?**
Yes. Daily consistent use is preferred for chronic conditions. CBD is not habit-forming.
