## What Lemonade Does NOT Cover

This is where honest reviews matter most. Lemonade excludes:

- **Pre-existing conditions.** Any illness or injury that showed symptoms before your start date is excluded. This includes conditions diagnosed during waiting periods.
- **Cosmetic procedures.** Ear cropping, tail docking, declawing.
- **Breeding and pregnancy.** Whelping complications, C-sections.
- **Preventive care** (without the add-on). Routine wellness exams, vaccines, annual dental cleanings.
- **Food and supplements** unless prescribed for a covered condition.
- **Behavioral training** as a standalone service (behavioral conditions are a separate add-on for underlying issues like anxiety).

### The Pre-Existing Condition Rule in Detail

Lemonade uses **retrospective underwriting** — meaning they review your dog's vet records at claim time, not at enrollment. This makes it fast to get started, but claims can be denied if medical records reveal a condition existed before your start date.

The exception: **curable pre-existing conditions** — things like ear infections, UTIs, or vomiting episodes — can be covered again after **12 months symptom-free and treatment-free.** This is a meaningful benefit for dogs with minor past health issues.

Chronic or incurable conditions (diabetes, allergies, epilepsy, heart disease) are excluded for life.

## The Claims Process: Where Lemonade Shines

Lemonade's claims process is genuinely differentiated. Most pet insurance claims involve:

1. Pay the vet out of pocket
2. Submit receipts and records
3. Wait 2–4 weeks for reimbursement

Lemonade's AI-first claims system can process and pay **most straightforward claims in minutes.** You submit a video explanation of the claim via app, attach the invoice, and the system flags it for either instant approval or human review.

In practice:
- Simple claims (a single vet visit for a clear diagnosis) are often approved within 24–48 hours
- Complex claims (cancer, orthopedic surgery, specialist care) still take 1–2 weeks and involve human review
- Claims that touch possible pre-existing conditions take longer as medical records are pulled

Customer reviews on claims experience are mixed but skew positive compared to traditional pet insurers. The most common complaint is denial due to pre-existing condition language in records.
