## #1 Embark — Best Overall Dog DNA Test

Embark is the gold standard for a reason. Founded by Cornell University geneticists, Embark tests over 230,000 genetic markers — more than twice what competitors test. That density matters for accuracy, especially for breed detection in dogs with complex mixed heritage.

### Breed Detection

Embark identifies breeds down to the 5% level and breaks results into "chromosomal segments" — you can see which chromosomes came from which breeds. For a mutt with 8+ breed contributors, this level of resolution is meaningfully more useful than a simple pie chart.

In independent accuracy testing, Embark consistently identifies rare and regional breeds that competitors miss, particularly for working breeds, sighthounds, and non-AKC-recognized breeds.

### Health Screening

This is where Embark truly separates. The 250+ health condition tests cover:
- **Drug sensitivities** (MDR1/ABCB1, BCRP, CYP1A2) — critical for collie-type breeds
- **Cardiac conditions** — DCM, ARVC, PDA
- **Eye conditions** — prcd-PRA, CEA, glaucoma predisposition
- **Bleeding disorders** — Von Willebrand's, Factor VII, Factor VIII
- **Metabolic disorders** — hyperuricosuria, cystinuria, copper toxicosis

Every test comes with a veterinarian-authored explanation of what the result means and what action to take. Results are shareable directly with your vet.

### Relatives Feature

Embark maintains a database of 2+ million tested dogs. If your dog has a genetically similar dog in the database, they appear as "relatives" — occasionally surfacing full siblings or parents from breeding programs. This is particularly useful for dogs adopted from high-volume shelters where litter-mates were separated.

- Most comprehensive health screening (250+ conditions)

- Active relatives network

## What Can Dog DNA Tests NOT Tell You?

- **Behavioral guarantees:** Breed composition influences behavior but doesn't determine it. Individual socialization, training, and experience matter more.
- **Disease diagnosis:** A positive health marker means elevated risk, not a guaranteed diagnosis. Consult your vet before drawing clinical conclusions.
- **Mixed breed accuracy:** Results become less precise as mixed heritage becomes more complex. Use results as useful approximations, not certainties.

## FAQ

### How accurate are dog DNA tests?

For dogs with clear breed heritage (purebred or F1 cross), accuracy is very high across all three platforms — 90%+ for major breed contributors. For complex multi-generational mixes, Embark's higher marker count produces meaningfully better resolution than competitors.

### How do I collect my dog's DNA sample?

All three tests use cheek swabs. Swab the inside of your dog's cheek for 30–60 seconds per swab. Avoid feeding for 30 minutes before collection. Let the swab air dry before sealing the package.

### Should I get the health screening add-on?

For large breeds, working breeds, or any breed with known genetic health vulnerabilities — yes, absolutely. The $100 price difference between breed-only and breed+health Embark kits is trivial against the cost of proactively managing a hereditary condition you didn't know your dog carried.

### Can I use my dog's DNA test results at the vet?

Yes. Embark and Wisdom Panel both generate veterinarian-formatted reports designed to be added to your dog's medical file. Some vets now request this proactively for breeds prone to drug sensitivities.

### Are dog DNA tests one-time or recurring?

One-time. The DNA doesn't change. Embark and Wisdom Panel do update their databases and may add new breed or health results to your account over time — one reason to create an account and keep your login.
