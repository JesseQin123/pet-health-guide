## Pet Stroller Buying Guide

### Weight Limit (the Most Important Spec)

Always buy a stroller rated for at least 20% more than your pet's current weight. Strollers operated consistently at their maximum rating wear out faster and become unsafe.

### Wheel Type Matters for Your Terrain

- **Hard plastic wheels**: Cheapest, fine for smooth sidewalks and store floors
- **Foam-filled rubber tires**: Better vibration absorption, no flats, good for most uses
- **Air-filled (pneumatic) tires**: Best ride quality, handles gravel and light trails, requires occasional inflation

### Canopy and Weather Protection

A zippered mesh window is the minimum — look for a canopy that can close completely for rain protection. Full weather covers (often sold separately on budget strollers, included on mid-range+) are worth having if you live somewhere with unpredictable weather.

### Foldability and Car Storage

Measure your trunk. Most strollers fold to roughly 30" x 15" x 12". Three-wheel strollers typically fold flatter than four-wheel designs and fit more easily in smaller vehicles.

### Entry and Exit Points

Dogs with mobility issues need low-entry or front-opening designs. A flip-down front bar or a bottom-loading entry lets your dog walk in without climbing. Rear-entry strollers are harder for dogs with hip dysplasia or joint pain.

## FAQ: Pet Strollers

**Do dogs actually like pet strollers?**
Most dogs adapt quickly, especially if introduced gradually with treats. Senior dogs and post-surgery dogs often take to strollers faster than healthy young dogs because they feel the relief immediately.

**Can I use a pet stroller for my cat?**
Yes — many cat owners use strollers for outdoor enrichment. Cats that enjoy "adventure cat" outings benefit from the security of an enclosed stroller on busy streets. Use a stroller with full mesh coverage so your cat can't escape.

**How do I introduce my dog to a stroller?**
Start with the stroller stationary. Let your dog investigate and reward calm behavior with treats. Once your dog is comfortable entering on their own, take short slow walks before graduating to longer outings.

**Are pet strollers airline-friendly?**
Standard pet strollers are checked or gate-checked at airlines — they're too large for cabin carry-on. The Ibiyaya 5-in-1's carrier function is airline-approved as cabin baggage for small pets under the seat.

**How do I clean a pet stroller?**
Most strollers have removable washable interiors. For the frame, wipe with a damp cloth. Check wheel bearings and frame joints periodically for dirt and lubricate moving parts with silicone spray.
