## Spot Pet Insurance Review

Spot launched in 2019 and has grown rapidly by offering flexible plan customization and one of the most competitive multi-pet discounts in the industry (10% per additional pet, uncapped).

### What Spot Covers

- Accidents and injuries (immediately after the 2-day waiting period)
- Illnesses including cancer, diabetes, and chronic conditions (after 14-day waiting period)
- Hereditary and congenital conditions (if not pre-existing at enrollment)
- Dental illnesses and periodontal disease
- Behavioral therapy (prescribed by a vet)
- Microchipping (in some plans)

### Spot's Plan Structure

Spot lets you choose:
- **Annual limit:** $2,500 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 / $7,000 / $10,000 / Unlimited
- **Annual deductible:** $100 / $250 / $500 / $1,000
- **Reimbursement rate:** 70% / 80% / 90%

The unlimited annual limit option is notable — Spot is one of few providers offering it, which matters for catastrophic illness cases like extended cancer treatment or multiple orthopedic surgeries.

### Spot Wellness Add-Ons

Spot's wellness plans (Gold: ~$10/mo, Platinum: ~$25/mo) cover routine vet exams, vaccines, flea/tick prevention, heartworm testing, and dental cleanings. These are true reimbursements — you pay your vet, submit a claim, and Spot reimburses up to your plan's benefit schedule.

### Spot's Weaknesses

- No accident-only plan option (competitors like Figo offer this)
- Orthopedic conditions have the same 14-day waiting period — shorter than Embrace, but orthopedic exclusions for pre-existing conditions remain strict
- No diminishing deductible feature (Embrace has this)
- Customer service response times received mixed reviews in 2025

**Best for:** Multi-pet households (10% per pet discount is the best in the industry); owners who want unlimited annual coverage; owners prioritizing a low orthopedic waiting period.

## Embrace Pet Insurance Review

Embrace has been in business since 2003, making it one of the oldest dedicated pet insurance companies in the U.S. It's known for its Healthy Pet Deductible feature and its coverage of alternative therapies without an add-on.

### What Embrace Covers

- Accidents and illnesses (after waiting periods)
- Hereditary and breed-specific conditions
- Dental illnesses (not just injuries)
- Chronic conditions including allergies and hypothyroidism
- Behavioral therapy
- **Alternative therapies:** Acupuncture, hydrotherapy, chiropractic, physical therapy — included in the base policy (competitors charge extra)
- Prescription medications

### Embrace's Plan Structure

- **Annual limit:** $5,000 / $8,000 / $10,000 / $15,000 / $30,000
- **Annual deductible:** $200 / $300 / $500 / $750 / $1,000
- **Reimbursement rate:** 70% / 80% / 90%

Embrace does not offer an unlimited annual limit — $30,000 is the ceiling.

### Healthy Pet Deductible

Embrace's most unique feature: the annual deductible decreases by $50 each year you don't file a claim, down to $0. This rewards healthy pets and owners who only use insurance for major events, potentially eliminating the deductible entirely after 10+ claim-free years.

### Embrace Wellness Rewards

Embrace's wellness add-on (Wellness Rewards) is more flexible than most competitors — it works as a spending account ($200–$650/year depending on tier) you can use on any preventive care item at any vet, including things most wellness plans won't cover like training classes, grooming, and doggy daycare.

### Embrace's Orthopedic Waiting Period

The biggest practical weakness of Embrace is its 6-month orthopedic waiting period for conditions like hip dysplasia, cruciate ligament tears, and intervertebral disc disease. This is the longest in the industry. It can be waived with an orthopedic exam performed by your vet before the policy starts — but that requires proactive action at enrollment.

**Best for:** Owners who use alternative therapies (acupuncture, hydrotherapy) and don't want to pay extra; owners who prefer plan longevity and the diminishing deductible; cat owners (Embrace typically prices lower for cats than Spot).

## Head-to-Head: Where Each Provider Wins

### Coverage: Tie (with caveats)

Both cover the same core categories. Embrace wins on alternative therapy (included vs. Spot's add-on). Spot wins on unlimited annual limits (Embrace caps at $30,000).

### Cost: Spot Slightly Cheaper on Average

Spot tends to run 5–15% cheaper than Embrace for equivalent plans on dogs. For cats, Embrace can be more competitive. Always get quotes from both — pricing varies significantly by breed, age, and zip code.

### Multi-Pet Households: Spot Wins Clearly

Spot's 10% per-pet discount applies to every additional pet with no cap. On a 3-pet household, that's a 20% savings on pets 2 and 3. Embrace's 5% discount is materially less valuable.

### Long-Term Policy Value: Embrace Wins

The Healthy Pet Deductible feature means that if your pet stays healthy for several years, your effective cost per claim drops. Spot has no equivalent feature.

### Orthopedic Conditions: Spot Wins

Spot's 14-day orthopedic waiting period is industry-standard. Embrace's 6-month wait is a real risk for large-breed dogs prone to cruciate ligament tears (Labs, Goldens, Rottweilers). If you have a large-breed dog, this is a significant consideration — unless you can arrange the waiver exam at enrollment.

### Claims Experience: Embrace Has a Longer Track Record

Embrace has been paying claims since 2003. Spot, launched in 2019, has less claims history data available. Embrace's claims turnaround time averages 10–15 business days; Spot averages similar but has received more complaints about denial rates on ambiguous pre-existing conditions.

## FAQ: Spot and Embrace Pet Insurance

**Do either Spot or Embrace cover pre-existing conditions?**
No — neither covers conditions that existed before the policy start date. This is standard across the pet insurance industry. Some curable pre-existing conditions may be reconsidered after 12 months claim-free.

**How long do claims take?**
Both typically process claims within 10–15 business days. Embrace offers a mobile app for claim submission; Spot also accepts claims via app or email.

**Do premiums increase with age?**
Yes — for both providers. Pet insurance premiums increase as your pet ages, typically more steeply after age 7–8. Locking in earlier saves money over time.

**Can I use any vet?**
Yes — both Spot and Embrace work with any licensed veterinarian in the U.S., including emergency clinics and specialists. You pay upfront and submit a claim for reimbursement.

**Is there a free look period?**
Both offer a 30-day money-back guarantee if you cancel before filing a claim.
