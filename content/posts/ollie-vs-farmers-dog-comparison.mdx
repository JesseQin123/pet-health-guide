## About Ollie

<a href="https://www.myollie.com" rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank">Ollie</a> launched in 2016 with a simple premise: feed dogs the way we'd want to be fed. Their recipes are developed by board-certified veterinary nutritionists and made with USDA-sourced ingredients — no fillers, no artificial preservatives, no rendered by-products.

What sets Ollie apart is variety. In addition to their four classic lightly cooked recipes (Beef with Sweet Potato, Chicken with Vegetables, Turkey with Sweet Potato, and Lamb with Vegetables), they offer baked kibble options for pet owners who want fresh-food quality in a more traditional dry format — at a lower price point.

**Ollie's best recipes:**
- **Hearty Beef** – grass-fed beef, sweet potatoes, carrots, peas, and spinach
- **Classic Chicken** – humanely raised chicken, chickpeas, carrots, and spinach
- **Saucy Turkey** – turkey, butternut squash, kale, and peas
- **Pork & Squash** – pork, squash, peas, spinach — a novel protein great for allergy-prone dogs

Each meal is portioned to your dog's exact caloric needs based on their profile. Ollie's app lets you pause, skip, or adjust deliveries easily, which pet owners consistently praise.

## About The Farmer's Dog

<a href="https://www.thefarmersdog.com" rel="nofollow sponsored" target="_blank">The Farmer's Dog</a> was founded in 2014 after co-founder Brett Podolsky struggled to find genuinely healthy food for his sick dog. Today it's one of the most recognizable names in fresh pet food, with millions of subscribers and a veterinary team that formulates every recipe to AAFCO standards.

The Farmer's Dog keeps their menu lean: four recipes, each using a single primary protein source with simple, recognizable vegetables and grains. Their beef recipe, for example, contains beef, sweet potato, and sunflower oil — that's it. No filler ingredients, no ingredient you can't pronounce.

**The Farmer's Dog recipes:**
- **Beef** – beef, sweet potato, sunflower oil
- **Chicken** – chicken, broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts
- **Turkey** – turkey, broccoli, cauliflower, Brussels sprouts, carrots
- **Pork** – pork, butternut squash, kale, Brussels sprouts, sunflower oil

The minimalist ingredient lists make The Farmer's Dog particularly appealing for dogs with food sensitivities, since there are fewer ingredients to trigger a reaction.

## Ingredients Quality

Both companies use human-grade, USDA-approved ingredients — meaning the same quality standards that apply to food for humans. Neither brand uses:

- Rendered meat by-products
- Artificial colors or flavors
- Chemical preservatives (BHA, BHT, ethoxyquin)
- Corn, wheat, or soy fillers

**Ollie's edge:** More recipe variety means more protein rotation options, which veterinary nutritionists often recommend to reduce the risk of developing food sensitivities over time.

**The Farmer's Dog's edge:** Shorter, simpler ingredient lists. If you want to know exactly what's in every bite, their transparency is unmatched. Their beef recipe has just three ingredients.

## Delivery & Packaging

Both services ship directly to your door in insulated boxes with dry ice or ice packs to keep food fresh during transit. Delivery experiences are generally positive for both companies.

**Ollie:** Ships every 2–8 weeks depending on your plan. Portions arrive in individual sealed pouches, which makes it easy to grab one meal at a time. The packaging is recyclable and compact.

**The Farmer's Dog:** Ships on a 4-week cycle (adjustable). Food arrives in portioned, pre-labeled packs organized by week, with instructions on how much to thaw and serve each day. The labeling system is intuitive for new subscribers.

Both companies let you store extra food in the freezer for up to 4 months.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is fresh dog food actually better than kibble?**
Research increasingly supports fresh, minimally processed diets for dogs. Studies show improvements in digestibility, stool quality, coat health, and energy levels compared to ultra-processed kibble. Both Ollie and The Farmer's Dog are AAFCO-compliant and formulated by veterinary nutritionists.

**Can I mix fresh food with my dog's regular kibble?**
Yes. Many owners use fresh food as a topper or mix it 50/50 with kibble to reduce cost while still improving diet quality. Both Ollie and The Farmer's Dog can work as a partial meal replacement.

**How do I transition my dog to fresh food?**
Both services recommend a gradual 7-day transition — starting with 25% fresh food mixed into current food, then increasing to 50%, 75%, and finally 100%. This reduces the risk of digestive upset.
