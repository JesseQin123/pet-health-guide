## How HolistaPet Works for Dogs

- Anxiety and stress reduction

HolistaPet's broad-spectrum formula includes minor cannabinoids like CBG and CBN, which may enhance the effect through what researchers call the **"entourage effect"** — the synergistic benefit of multiple hemp compounds working together.
