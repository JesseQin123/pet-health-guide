## Brand Overview

### HolistaPet

HolistaPet launched in 2017 with a mission to bring human-grade CBD quality standards to the pet industry. Their products use organically grown Colorado hemp and offer a wide range of potencies — from entry-level 150mg tinctures for small dogs all the way to 3,000mg options for large breeds or dogs with serious health challenges.

What sets HolistaPet apart is their commitment to full-spectrum formulations. Full-spectrum CBD retains minor cannabinoids, terpenes, and flavonoids that work together through what's called the "entourage effect" — potentially amplifying the benefits of CBD beyond what isolate products offer.

**Best for:** Dog owners who want the most potent, full-spectrum option with a wide product lineup.

### Innovet

Innovet launched in 2016 and quickly became known for their PurCBD line. Unlike HolistaPet, Innovet focuses primarily on CBD isolate formulations — meaning they strip out all other hemp compounds and deliver pure CBD only. This appeals to owners concerned about trace THC exposure, especially in states with strict regulations or for dogs that undergo drug testing.

Innovet's price points tend to run slightly lower, making them an accessible entry point for first-time CBD buyers.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious owners or those who specifically want a THC-free isolate product.
