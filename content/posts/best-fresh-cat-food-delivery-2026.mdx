## Why Fresh Cat Food Matters

Cats evolved eating prey animals that are roughly 70% water. Dry kibble averages only 8–10% moisture. This chronic dehydration contributes to urinary tract disease, kidney disease, and bladder crystals — some of the most common and expensive health problems cats face.

Fresh food and high-quality wet food keep cats properly hydrated, support kidney function, and deliver protein in a bioavailable form their digestive systems evolved to use.

**The investment:** Fresh cat food costs $2–$6/day versus $0.50–$1.50/day for premium kibble. But one urinary obstruction surgery can cost $1,500–$3,000. Many cat owners find the upgrade pays for itself.
