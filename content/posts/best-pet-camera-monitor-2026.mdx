## What to Look for in a Pet Camera

**Resolution:** 1080p is the minimum worth buying in 2026. 2K and above is ideal.

**Field of view:** 130° is narrow (fine for one corner of a room), 160°+ is better for open floor plans.

**Treat dispenser:** Only needed if you want interactive engagement. Skip it if you mainly want to watch.

**Night vision:** Essential — dogs are most active during early morning and evening hours when lighting is low.

**Subscription:** Furbo and Petcube both offer free basic functionality but charge for cloud history and AI alerts. Eufy and Pawbo offer full features without subscriptions.

**Two-way audio:** Standard on all quality pet cameras. Your voice calming an anxious dog remotely is genuinely effective.
