## What to Look for in CBD Dog Treats

Before diving into picks, here's what separates quality products from overpriced candy:

### 1. Verified CBD Content (COA)
A Certificate of Analysis from an independent, ISO-certified lab is non-negotiable. It confirms:
- Actual CBD content matches label claim
- No THC above 0.3%
- No heavy metals, pesticides, or microbial contamination

### 2. CBD Spectrum
- **Broad-spectrum**: Multiple cannabinoids and terpenes, but no THC. Preferred for most dogs.
- **Full-spectrum**: Includes trace THC (<0.3%). Some owners prefer this for the "entourage effect."
- **Isolate**: Pure CBD, no other cannabinoids. Least effective per dose.

### 3. Meaningful Dose Per Treat
Many brands load treats with 1–2 mg CBD per treat. For a 50 lb dog, you'd need 5–10 treats per dose. Look for **5 mg or more per treat** for practical dosing.

### 4. Clean Ingredient List
Avoid: artificial colors, artificial preservatives (BHA, BHT), corn syrup, soy, excessive fillers.
Look for: real food ingredients, named protein source, natural preservatives.

### 5. Formulation Purpose
The best brands combine CBD with synergistic ingredients:
- **For anxiety**: L-theanine, chamomile, passionflower
- **For joints**: Glucosamine, chondroitin, boswellia
- **For immune health**: CoQ10, antioxidants

## How to Dose CBD Dog Treats

Starting dose: **0.25 mg CBD per pound of body weight**, twice daily.

| Dog Weight | Starting Dose | Treats Needed (5mg/treat) |
|-----------|--------------|--------------------------|
| 10 lbs | 2.5 mg | 0.5 treat (break in half) |
| 25 lbs | 6.25 mg | 1.5 treats |
| 50 lbs | 12.5 mg | 2.5 treats |
| 75 lbs | 18.75 mg | 4 treats |
| 100 lbs | 25 mg | 5 treats |

Adjust after 2–4 weeks based on response. Most dogs reach their optimal dose between 0.25–0.5 mg/lb twice daily.

## Are CBD Dog Treats Safe?

CBD has an excellent safety profile in dogs. A 2020 Colorado State University study found no adverse effects at doses up to 4.5 mg/kg in dogs. The most common side effect is **mild drowsiness** (usually a sign you've found the right dose) and occasionally loose stools when first introducing.

**Important**: Keep CBD treats away from children and never give products containing THC. THC is toxic to dogs — always verify your product contains less than 0.3% THC and is labeled for pets.
