## Dog Bike Trailer Buying Guide

### Weight Capacity: Account for the Gear

The listed weight capacity is for the dog alone — not the dog plus water, food, blanket, or toys. Subtract ~5 lbs from the listed capacity to account for gear if you're packing anything inside.

### Wheel Size Matters for Comfort

**20-inch wheels:** Better stability, rolls over road imperfections with less jarring. Best for trails, mixed terrain, or rough pavement.

**16-inch wheels:** Lighter, easier to store. Fine for smooth bike paths and paved roads. Noticeable bounce on gravel or rough pavement.

If you ride trails or live somewhere with pothole-heavy pavement, the Burley's 20-inch wheels are worth the price premium.

### Hitch Compatibility

Most trailers use a universal hitch that attaches to the rear axle. Fat bikes, recumbent bikes, carbon frames with fragile dropouts, and some e-bikes may not be compatible with standard hitches. Check your bike's rear axle diameter and frame material before ordering.

### Air-Filled vs. Foam-Filled Tires

Air-filled tires ride smoother and absorb shock better. They can go flat. Foam-filled (no-flat) tires never go flat but transmit more road vibration to your dog. For short recreational rides, foam-filled is lower maintenance. For longer or rougher rides, air is better.

### Safety Essentials

- **Always use the internal leash:** Even calm dogs can bolt at sudden sounds. The internal leash prevents exit but allows position changes.
- **Introduce gradually:** Let your dog sniff and explore the trailer before the first ride. Feed meals inside with the door open before ever attaching it to a bike.
- **Check the hitch before every ride:** Metal fatigue on the hitch connection point is the primary failure mode in lower-quality trailers.
- **Bring water:** Dogs in trailers still pant and get hot. Bring a collapsible bowl and stop for water on rides over 30 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**At what age can puppies ride in a bike trailer?**

Most experts recommend waiting until 6 months (once vaccinations are complete and the puppy's skeletal structure is more developed). Keep initial rides short — under 20 minutes — to avoid motion sickness in young dogs.

**Do dogs get motion sick in bike trailers?**

Some do, especially initially. Signs: drooling, whimpering, reluctance to enter. Combat it by keeping the dog facing forward (not backward), using a car-sickness remedy (ginger dog treats, or ask your vet about Cerenia), and keeping initial rides under 10 minutes.

**Can I use a kid's bike trailer for my dog?**

Yes — structurally, most child trailers are identical or superior to dog trailers. The key differences: child trailers lack internal leash anchors, and some are narrower. An adapter leash can solve the first issue. Check internal dimensions against your dog's size.

**What's the speed limit for dog trailers?**

There's no official limit, but most trailers are rated for recreational cycling speeds (under 20 mph). At higher speeds, the trailer's sway becomes more pronounced and harder to manage. Keep it under 15 mph on trails with a dog on board.
