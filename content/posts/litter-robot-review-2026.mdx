## What the Litter-Robot 4 Does Well

### Actual odor elimination

The Litter-Robot 4's biggest real-world improvement over previous generations is the OdorTrap system — a rubber seal on the waste drawer and an upgraded carbon filter that seals in odors between empties. In our 3-cat house, the smell dropped dramatically within the first week. Guests stopped noticing "the cat room." That alone is worth something.

The globe cycling away clumps within minutes of a cat exiting means waste isn't sitting in the box, off-gassing. Traditional boxes trap odor for hours between scoopings.

### App monitoring and health insights

The Litter-Robot app has gotten genuinely useful. It logs every visit per cat (it distinguishes cats by weight), giving you a graph of litter box usage over time. As a cat health tool, this is underrated — sudden changes in visit frequency or duration are early signals of urinary issues, the leading cause of emergency vet visits in cats. 

We got an alert when one cat's average visit time increased by 40% over a week — a visit to the vet revealed early signs of a UTI. That's $700 paying for itself in a different way than expected.

### Convenience (it's real)

We went from scooping twice a day to emptying a waste drawer once every 8–10 days. The time savings compound: that's roughly 30–40 minutes per week per multi-cat household. Over a year, you're looking at 26–35 hours of scooping you didn't do.

## Where the Litter-Robot 4 Falls Short

### The price is genuinely steep

$699 is a significant purchase. Litter-Robot offers financing, but let's be honest: it's still a premium commitment. If you have one cat and it's manageable with daily scooping, the ROI case is harder to make.

### Clumping clay litter only

The Litter-Robot requires clumping clay litter, which eliminates silica crystal litters, pellet litters, and most eco-friendly options. If you've already transitioned your cats to a non-clumping alternative, you'll need to switch back.

### Large footprint

The globe-shaped unit is large — bigger than most standard litter boxes. It needs clear space to cycle. In small apartments, this can be a real constraint.

### Cat acceptance isn't guaranteed

Some cats — particularly shy or older ones — refuse to enter the Litter-Robot for weeks. Litter-Robot's training guide works, but there's no guarantee your cat will accept it. If they don't, the return window matters (Litter-Robot offers a 90-day money-back guarantee for exactly this reason).

## Setup and Real-World Use

Setup takes about 20 minutes. The unit ships partially assembled — you attach the globe, fill with clumping litter, and connect to WiFi via the app. The initial onboarding includes a test cycle you run manually before your cats ever use it.

Getting cats comfortable typically takes 3–7 days. Place it near their old litter box initially, and don't remove the old box until they're using the Litter-Robot consistently.

The waste drawer holds roughly 10 days of clumps for one cat, 5–7 days for two, and 3–5 days for three cats. Liners make emptying clean and fast.
