## How to Choose the Right Supplement for Your Senior Dog

### By severity of symptoms:

| Symptom Level | Recommended Approach |
|---|---|
| Preventive (no symptoms yet, over age 6) | Omega-3 fish oil + Cosequin or Zesty Paws |
| Mild stiffness (slow to rise, hesitates on stairs) | Zesty Paws Mobility Bites + Omega-3 |
| Moderate arthritis (limping, reduced activity) | Dasuquin + Omega-3 + consider HolistaPet CBD |
| Severe arthritis (pain on palpation, won't use limb) | Dasuquin + Omega-3 + HolistaPet CBD + vet consultation for additional treatment |

### Signs your dog may have joint pain:

- Reluctance to climb stairs or jump onto furniture
- Stiffness after rest, especially in cold weather
- Licking or chewing at joints
- Visible limping or altered gait
- Personality changes (withdrawal, irritability)
- Reduced interest in walks or play

If you're seeing these signs, schedule a vet visit. X-rays can confirm arthritis and help your vet recommend the right combination of supplements, NSAIDs, physical therapy, or laser treatment.

## What the Science Says

**Glucosamine and chondroitin:** A 2007 study in JAVMA found that dogs with hip dysplasia showed significant improvement in pain scores after 70 days on glucosamine/chondroitin. Multiple subsequent studies support their use for moderate osteoarthritis.

**Omega-3 fatty acids:** A 2010 study (JAVMA) found dogs with osteoarthritis who received omega-3-enriched food showed improved weight-bearing and reduced veterinary pain scores at 6 and 12 weeks.

**CBD:** A 2018 Cornell study (Front. Vet. Sci.) found 80% of arthritic dogs showed significant improvement in pain and mobility on 2mg/kg twice-daily CBD oil. No adverse effects were observed.

The most effective approach for most senior dogs with joint disease is **layered supplementation** — glucosamine/chondroitin as the foundation, omega-3s for inflammation, and CBD for pain management and quality of life.
