## How Innovet CBD Works

CBD interacts with receptors in the **endocannabinoid system (ECS)** — a biological system that regulates inflammation, pain signaling, mood, and immune response. Dogs (and all mammals) have a densely distributed ECS, making CBD particularly well-suited to managing:

- **Chronic pain**: Including arthritis and neuropathy
- **Anxiety**: Separation anxiety, noise phobias, travel stress
- **Inflammatory conditions**: Skin allergies, IBD, interstitial cystitis
- **Seizures**: Innovet was one of the early brands used in anecdotal epilepsy management (note: not a replacement for prescribed anticonvulsants)
- **Nausea**: Common in dogs undergoing chemotherapy or with vestibular disease

## How to Dose Innovet CBD Oil

Innovet provides a weight-based dosing calculator on their website, but a general framework:

- **Starter dose**: 0.25 mg CBD per pound of body weight daily
- **Standard dose**: 0.5 mg CBD per pound daily
- **High dose**: 1 mg CBD per pound daily (for severe conditions)

Start at the low end and increase every 5–7 days until you observe the desired effect. Administer directly into the mouth via dropper or mix into food.

## FAQ

### Is Innovet CBD oil safe for dogs?
Yes. Innovet's CBD oil is tested for safety and is formulated specifically for dogs. Always check with your vet before starting CBD, especially if your dog takes other medications (CBD can affect how the liver metabolizes some drugs).

### Does Innovet CBD oil contain THC?
PurCBD+ contains trace amounts of THC (under 0.3%), which is legal federally and safe for most dogs. PurHemp+ is THC-free for owners who prefer zero THC.

### How long before I see results from Innovet?
Acute anxiety: 30–60 minutes. Chronic conditions (arthritis, skin issues): 2–6 weeks of daily use.

### Can I use Innovet CBD alongside my dog's prescriptions?
Potentially, but consult your vet first. CBD is processed by the same liver enzymes as many medications and can affect dosing requirements.

### Is Innovet good for cats too?
Yes — the 75 mg formula is specifically sized for cats and small dogs. Do not use dog formulas for cats without vet guidance on dose adjustment.
