## Tips for Buying Budget CBD Without Sacrificing Safety

**Always check for a COA.** A Certificate of Analysis from an independent lab is non-negotiable. If a brand doesn't publish them or makes you jump through hoops to find them, skip it.

**Potency matters for value.** A $20 bottle with 100mg of CBD isn't better value than a $30 bottle with 250mg. Calculate cost per mg.

**Subscribe-and-save programs.** HolistaPet (25% off) and Innovet (20% off) both offer subscription discounts that push already-budget products into excellent value territory.
