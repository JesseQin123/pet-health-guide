## How to Choose CBD Oil for Your Dog

### 1. Check for Third-Party Testing
Never buy CBD without a Certificate of Analysis (COA) from an independent lab. It should confirm CBD content and show THC below 0.3%.

### 2. Choose the Right Spectrum
- **Full-spectrum**: Contains all cannabinoids including trace THC (most effective — "entourage effect")
- **Broad-spectrum**: THC-removed, still contains other cannabinoids
- **Isolate**: Pure CBD only, no other cannabinoids

### 3. Calculate the Right Dose
General starting point: **0.2mg CBD per pound of body weight**, twice daily. For a 50-lb dog: 10mg per dose.

See our full [CBD dosage guide by weight](/blog/cbd-dosage-dogs-weight-chart) for a complete chart.

### 4. Watch for Side Effects
CBD is generally well-tolerated. Possible side effects include mild sedation, dry mouth, or lowered blood pressure. Start low and increase gradually.
